About Me

My work in stock systems and data

I’m Inna Bains, a Stock Systems Administrator and MSc Data Science student. My work involves tracing stock movements and investigating differences between the warehouse system and physical stock.

Current Experience

I review transactions in JDA, check stock locations and help resolve inventory discrepancies. I also record investigation outcomes and outstanding checks for the next shift.

My Background

Before working in stock systems, I supervised production teams, monitored performance and quality, and trained new staff. I graduated from Arden University in 2026 with First Class Honours in Computing and have since started an MSc in Data Science.

What I Have Built

For my Computing dissertation, I built a meal-planning application that combines nutritional data, estimated carbon emissions and pantry stock to recommend meals. I also built a PHP and MySQL travel challenge game and designed a relational database for a vehicle marketplace.
